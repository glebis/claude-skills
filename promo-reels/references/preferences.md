# Preferences

The user's taste and standing decisions. Defaults in SKILL.md yield to these; ask before
overriding one. New entries come from `learn.py add --kind preference`.

## Seed (first project, a documentary comedy theatre show — project-specific items marked *)
- Structure: hook slide first (with title, date, venue and a "buy a ticket" button on it), footage, CTA slide last.
- Typography must be proportional/algorithmic (fill-to-width + modular scale), never "shrink until it fits".
- Deliver every variant asked for, not a pick: all cuts × all music tracks, named `<cut>__<track>.mp4`.
- Offer two paces: a dynamic cut and a calm one cut to the music's bars.
- *Style: `doc-red` (matched to the venue's site). For new projects ask — don't default to it.
- *Music direction that landed: nostalgic 1970s comedy film score — xylophone, jazz rhythm section, minor key.
- Licensed songs (e.g. a classic glam-rock single) are cut on their tempo grid and added in Instagram, not embedded.
- Captions only from verified speech; otherwise documentary stage directions.
