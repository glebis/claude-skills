# Perception and pacing rules (vertical reels, muted autoplay)

- **Reading load:** total on-screen words ≤ ~2–2.5 words/s per scene (Russian/English). If above, cut copy before speeding motion.
- **Split attention:** do not start revealing the second text block while the first is still being read. Quote starts after context ≈ 6 frames/context word (14–32 frames).
- **Word reveal:** silent reading 3.3–4.2 words/s is the upper bound; reveal at 8–15 frames/word, finish by ~70 % of the scene, then hold the full sentence ≥ 0.5 s before the exit.
- **Event boundaries:** big changes (card, cut, music downbeat, hit) should coincide — they segment the story. Cut on downbeats.
- **Cards/flash text:** ≥ 0.6 s hold (0.3 s is unreadable). Text that flashes shorter is perceived as noise.
- **One focal motion at a time:** art enters with the scene, then drifts slowly (≤ 3–6 % over the scene) so it doesn't compete with reading. Pointing devices (manicule, arrow) may move — they direct attention to the text.
- **Narrative:** context lines form one causal chain (setup → tension → turn → question), each line linking to the next ("…, но", "А …", "Дальше — вопрос:"). A list of topics is not a narrative.
- **Quotes:** verbatim only; trim at start/end; at most one internal […]; never add words; never present expectations as results.
- **Safe areas (IG):** keep text between y ≈ 0.11 and 0.78; avoid the right ~10 % in the lower half; art may bleed off edges.
- **Voice-over** (optional): narrating identical on-screen text competes with reading (redundancy effect). Prefer: no VO, or VO for editorial lines only with quotes silent. Duck music ~11 dB under VO.
