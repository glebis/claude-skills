# Interaction choices

The habit to build is asking “what could these capabilities do together?” Each choice below serves that habit, or protects it from a score, a catalogue or a false claim.

## Choices

**Shape means type.** Square is text, diamond is a record, circle is audio. When shapes match, a hop needs nothing in between. When they do not, you can see the missing middle. The lesson is carried by the picture, so nobody has to read a rule.

**Your idea comes first, and nothing grades it.** Examples stay hidden until you write or sketch something. The hop panel also hides the rule’s middle until then, so it cannot leak the answer. There is no skip, but one word counts.

**Variety, not volume.** A route can have two to five parts, in five shapes. Draw deals 2, 3, 4 or a surprise number. Tried shapes get a small dot, and one line invites an untried shape. Nothing counts ideas. Taking a part away is an equal move: “Shortest route” asks which part you could not remove.

**Honest gaps.** Some routes have no rule, and the app says so. The practical plank disappears rather than inventing a bridge. A recorded gap, such as Health to SQLite, stays visibly broken.

**Cuts stay visible.** A constraint moves a bridge to a list with its reason, so you see what the constraint cost. Where a seed has a local version, it is rewritten and labelled.

**Gates stay separate.** Nous shows catalog, paid access, connector rollout, free tool pool and connected accounts as separate rows. A catalog of 54 is never shown as 54 connections.

**The distance rule is exposed.** “Distant” means a different domain and a different place. It is a true-or-false sorting rule, not a score, and “These aren’t distant to me” overrules it.

**The route owns its space.** The stack rail and every panel share one side column, so a panel replaces the rail and never takes width from the route. Cards then choose full, compact or stacked layouts from the width they have.

**Every step can be handed off.** Whatever you are looking at becomes a prompt: the stack, a selection, a route, a hop, a part, a bridge, an unlock, a swap or a saved experiment. Each has three jobs. Sources reads documentation and answers the open questions. Ideas generates varied options in different shapes. Build makes the smallest test, or proposes one and waits for a go-ahead. Every prompt carries the evidence, what is unknown and your constraints, and none of them score anything.

**Handoffs keep the habit.** An Ideas prompt made before you have put your own idea down tells the agent to ask for yours first. A route prompt hides the rule's middle until you have written your idea, as the canvas does. A blocked hop stays blocked, and a build prompt tells the agent to stop there.

**Money is not the default answer.** Unlocks separates new capability, more quota, free grants and glue. The cheapest unlock in the sample has no new fee, and costs an evening.

## Limits

- Five parts per route. A longer route may be two routes, and the interface says so.
- While a panel is open, the rail is covered. Close the panel, or use Parts, to add a part.
- The lens and view specs work on the stack only. On a route, the same evidence is on each part.
- Rule-made text is template text. It reads plainly, but it knows nothing about you, and it is labelled that way.
