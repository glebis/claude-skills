# Open Knowledge Vault product direction

Product: a portable knowledge workspace for people and agents, with setup and ongoing source-grounded curation. The core is OKF; optional adapters include Obsidian, Dataview, capture integrations, and website rendering.

Initial agent-plugin scope: initialise, inspect, curate, index, audit, and optionally install a chosen Dataview build. No unattended LLM worker, hosted sync, payment system, public marketplace release, or Obsidian runtime plugin is included in v0.1.0.

Commercial proposal, not a launched offer:
- Open starter: format profile, setup, structural checks, basic indexes
- Paid setup/migration: map an existing corpus, connect authorised sources, configure useful views, train the team
- Paid maintained workspace: freshness review, conflict resolution, curation, and operational support with explicit data boundaries
- Team packages: domain templates, agreed terminology, role-specific maps, approval workflow, and integrations

Charge for the implementation and service, not exclusive access to an open standard. A dashboard alone is unlikely to be sufficient differentiation. Validate willingness to pay against a concrete recurring problem: recovering decisions, maintaining project context, or making meeting knowledge reusable.

Do not imply Google affiliation, official certification, format ownership, measured ROI, or available service guarantees. Choose product licensing and review third-party licensing before distribution; do not rebundle a personal Dataview data.json. Packaging here is local and does not publish or bill anyone.
